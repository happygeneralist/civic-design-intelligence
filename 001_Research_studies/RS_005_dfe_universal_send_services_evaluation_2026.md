# Universal SEND Services process and impact evaluation (RR1589)

## Purpose

Contrast local inspection evidence with a national professional-development programme and preserve the difference between staff perceptions and pupil outcomes.

## Method and source voice

CFE Research mixed-method programme evaluation for DfE; management information, delegate survey, interviews and reflective diaries. The evaluator reports staff responses and researcher synthesis. This is primary research with professionals as published in a secondary report; it is not direct lived experience from children or families.
