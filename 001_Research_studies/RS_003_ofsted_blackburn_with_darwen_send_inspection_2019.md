# Joint local area SEND inspection in Blackburn with Darwen

## Purpose

Test whether effective professional coordination and lengthy service waits can coexist in one local system.

## Method and source voice

Joint Ofsted/CQC local-area inspection; interviews, provider visits, records and performance-data review. Institutional inspection synthesis includes reported family/professional views. It is not a direct transcript or statistically representative family study.
