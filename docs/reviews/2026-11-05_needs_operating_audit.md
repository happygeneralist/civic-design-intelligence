## Findings and corrections

| Finding | Consequence | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The broad vision already supports fast creation, stable knowledge and human/civic outcomes, but active instructions vary. | Agents can follow a narrow local rule and miss the governing intent. | One public semantic contract, linked from entry points and agent instructions, with explicit precedence for the relevant conflicts. |
| The roadmap still described the July single-source slice as the next task. | Repeated tiny ingestion experiments could displace the actual pilot. | Current archive/analysis checkpoint and ACL calibration → CDI #99 sequencing, without another architecture prerequisite. |
| Quality rules judged coherence by one solution class and directed splitting/narrowing whenever evidence was partial. | Legitimate broad or assumptive needs could be suppressed or prematurely altered. | Coherent human progress, independently meaningful constituents, retained parents, explicit partial support and omission checks. |
| A single needs ladder and `solution_requirement` examples conflicted with actual allowed need-level values. | Mechanisms could become needs, or useful cross-links be forced into a tree. | Graph semantics and documented levels reconciled with existing validator/templates; no new type or mandatory field. |
| Canonical wording, authentic language and role-specific views were insufficiently separated. | New renders could silently change meaning or look like participant quotations. | Persisted current formulation, short affordance, labelled interpretation and revision-bound views. |
| Need, pain, value, fulfilment, indicators and rights could be conflated. | Process completion or efficiency could substitute for human outcomes. | Explicit roles, expected/realised distinctions, and empirical/normative/legal bases. |
| Validator required exactly three numeric digits. | `UN_1000` and a larger corpus would fail before research could scale. | At least three ASCII digits; old IDs unchanged, plus regression coverage through 10,000. |
| Reviewed declarations could contradict `human_reviewed`. | Structurally inconsistent review claims could pass. | Error checks for declared-state inconsistency; these still cannot establish that human review actually occurred. |
| Existing needs claim direct evidence fit while recording no evidence basis. | A reader or agent could mistake a working assumption for direct support. | Advisory warning and explicit review queue, not automatic rewriting or invented evidence. |
